Every night, little Mira sat on her rooftop, watching the stars shimmer like diamonds scattered across velvet. She always wished she could keep just one. One evening, as she whispered her usual wish, a tiny glowing creature landed beside her. It looked like a firefly but brighter—almost like a star itself. “I’m Lumo,” it chirped. “I collect lost stars. Want to help?” Mira’s eyes widened. “Yes!”
